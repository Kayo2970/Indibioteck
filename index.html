Who We Are

Science-Driven.
Impact-Focused.

"Where Science Meets Sustainability"

Indibiotek Private Limited is a dynamic biotechnology enterprise headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, with a pan-India presence spanning five regional offices. We operate at the intersection of cutting-edge research and real-world impact.

From drug discovery to sustainable agriculture, our multidisciplinary team is committed to developing solutions that address the most pressing challenges in life sciences, food security, and environmental health.

Mission

To deliver transformative biotechnology solutions that improve lives and protect our planet through rigorous R&D and strategic partnerships.

Vision

To become India's most trusted biotech innovation hub, recognised globally for excellence in life sciences and sustainable development.

Research & Development

Our R&D Facilities

State-of-the-art research centres collaborating with premier academic institutions to accelerate innovation.

C4D Centre

Centre for Drug Design Discovery & Development

SRM University, Delhi-NCR, Sonipat

A dedicated facility focused on the full spectrum of drug discovery — from computational modelling and hit identification to lead optimisation and preclinical validation.

  • Computational drug design & molecular docking
  • Medicinal chemistry & synthesis
  • In vitro and in vivo validation studies
  • Academic-industry collaborative research
E-YUVA Centre

E-YUVA Entrepreneurship & Innovation Hub

Adamas University, Kolkata

Supported by BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council), this centre fosters bio-entrepreneurship, startup incubation and translational research among emerging scientists.

  • BIRAC-supported innovation ecosystem
  • Startup incubation & mentorship
  • Translational research programmes
  • Young entrepreneur development
