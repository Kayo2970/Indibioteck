Who We Are

Science-Driven.
India-Rooted.

"Indigenous Innovation, Global Standard Solutions"

Indibiotek Private Limited is a DPIIT-recognised biotech startup headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal. Founded in 2024 and registered in 2025, we develop innovative biotechnology products across three core divisions: Lifesciences, Agri, and Scientific.

From AI/ML-powered portable diagnostics and CRISPR-based detection to sustainable agri-biotech solutions, our multidisciplinary team is committed to making global-standard innovations accessible across India and beyond.

Mission

Use biotechnology to improve the health and well-being of people worldwide, developing innovative solutions to the most pressing healthcare and sustainability challenges.

Vision

To be India's leading biotech innovation company, recognised globally for excellence in diagnostics, agri-solutions, and scientific instrumentation.

What We Build

Our Three Core Divisions

Pioneering biotech solutions across diagnostics, agriculture, and scientific instrumentation with globally competitive standards.

Life Sciences

AI/ML-powered portable diagnostics and molecular detection technologies for human health and food security.

Our flagship ASSURED device integrates LAMP amplification, microfluidics, and CRISPR/Cas detection — enabling singlex and multiplex diagnostics across human health, food safety, dairy, veterinary, and aquaculture applications.

LAMP Technology CRISPR/Cas Detection Microfluidics Diagnostic Kits Reagents & Buffers

Agri

Sustainable bio-based agricultural solutions designed to boost crop yield while protecting soil health and ecosystems.

Our bio-agriculture range replaces chemical inputs with safer, effective biological alternatives — from biofertilisers that enrich soil microbiomes to CRISPR-informed crop protection technologies.

Biofertilisers Bioinsecticides Biopesticides Biofungicides PGR & Bio-Organics

Scientific

High-quality laboratory instruments, medical devices, labwares, and biomedical waste management systems.

We supply precision lab equipment and medical instruments alongside our patented autonomous robotic biomedical waste classification system — driving safety and efficiency in lab and clinical environments.

Lab Equipment Medical Instruments Labwares & Consumables Waste Management Robotic Automation
Research & Development

Our R&D Centres

Two dedicated R&D facilities at India's leading universities, powering the science behind our innovations.

C4D Centre

Centre for Drug Design, Discovery & Development

SRM University · Delhi-NCR Campus, Sonipat

Our flagship drug discovery facility at SRM University drives the full pipeline — from computational modelling and molecular target identification through to lead compound optimisation and preclinical validation.

  • Computational drug design & molecular docking
  • Medicinal chemistry synthesis & SAR studies
  • In vitro and in vivo preclinical validation
  • Collaborative academic-industry research
E-YUVA Centre

E-YUVA Entrepreneurship & Innovation Hub

Adamas University · Kolkata, West Bengal

Backed by BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council), the E-YUVA centre is a launchpad for biotech entrepreneurs — incubating the next generation of life sciences innovators from campus to market.

  • BIRAC-supported innovation ecosystem
  • Biotech startup incubation & seed mentorship
  • Translational research programmes
  • Young entrepreneur development & training
